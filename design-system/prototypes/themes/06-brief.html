Monthly review

The year is on track, one category is not

Sales reached 841.8M through August, 1.5% ahead of the same months last year.

Against plan the picture is tighter: 95.8% attainment, 37.2M short. Four categories beat their target; Electronics missed by 44.9M on its own, more than the whole gap.

Store performance is uneven. Ilsan is down 38.6% on last year, Jeju 29.7%, Ulsan 24.9%.
